Method

Here, every number has a source

Every great structure comes with a legend: the worker buried in the concrete, the structure “built in X days”, the death toll nobody checked. Every figure on this site carries a seal saying how far it can be trusted.

When the sources disagree, we show the versions and do not pick one. When nobody recorded it, we say so instead of making it up.

  • 2+ sources

    ConfirmedConfirmed in two or more independent sources.

  • 1 source

    Single sourceA single source; the text says which.

  • disputed

    DisagreementThe sources disagree; we show the versions and do not pick one.

  • no record

    No recordNobody recorded it. We say so instead of making it up.
